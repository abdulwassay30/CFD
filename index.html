Selected projects

Fluid mechanics and thermoelectric sensing.

Two projects showing how I use simulation to investigate mixing performance and coupled thermal electrical behaviour.

01

ANSYS Fluent • Computational Fluid Dynamics

Mixing patterns in anaerobic digesters

A CFD study comparing mixing behaviour in anaerobic digesters with different tank shapes and internal geometries. The analysis focused on velocity distribution, circulation zones and regions with weak mixing.

Model setup

  • Steady state pressure based solver
  • Realizable k ε turbulence model
  • Non Newtonian fluid rheology
  • Rotating helical impeller and draft tube

Engineering purpose

  • Compare vessel mixing patterns
  • Identify circulation and low velocity zones
  • Assess impeller and draft tube influence
  • Support digester geometry selection
02

COMSOL Multiphysics • Coupled Physics

Printed thermoelectric thermocouple response

A coupled model used to describe how infrared heating produces a temperature distribution and an electrical potential across a printed p type and n type thermocouple.

Physics included

  • Surface to surface radiation
  • Heat transfer in solids
  • Electric currents
  • Thermoelectric effect

Study outputs

  • Temperature distribution under laser heating
  • Electric potential across the junction
  • Potential variation through the z direction
  • Comparison with experimental voltage
